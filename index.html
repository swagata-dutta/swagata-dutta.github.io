Research Interests

My research is focused on uncovering deep structural properties of graphs using algebraic and spectral methods.

Spectral Graph Theory

Investigating eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and spectra of graph matrices (adjacency, Laplacian, signless Laplacian) to deduce connectivity, bipartiteness, and topology.

Permanental Polynomials

Analyzing properties of permanental polynomials, root distribution, coefficient behavior, and characterizing which graphs are uniquely determined by their polynomials.
